# Collection SEO Checklist

- Unique collection title and H1.
- Category-intent intro content.
- Crawlable product grid.
- Internal links to subcategories and related guides.
- Filtering indexation strategy.
- Sorting URL strategy.
- Pagination crawlability.
- Canonical behavior.
- Duplicate collection risk.
- Collection schema or breadcrumb requirements.
- Owner named for implementation.
