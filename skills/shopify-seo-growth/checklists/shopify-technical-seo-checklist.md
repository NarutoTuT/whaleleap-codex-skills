# Shopify Technical SEO Checklist

- Crawl important product, collection, page, and article URLs.
- Confirm primary pages are indexable.
- Check robots.txt strategy.
- Check sitemap inclusion.
- Check canonical tags.
- Check hreflang if international.
- Review collection pagination.
- Review faceted/filter URL behavior.
- Identify duplicate product URL paths.
- Check internal link depth.
- Confirm server-rendered access to important content.
- Validate structured data errors.
- Prioritize findings by business impact and implementation owner.
