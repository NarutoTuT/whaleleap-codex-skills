# GEO and AI Search Framework

## Scope

Use for Generative Engine Optimization, AI Search visibility, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, AI crawler accessibility, and llms.txt strategy.

## AI Search Readiness

Evaluate whether the store provides clear, extractable, factual information:

- Product names, categories, specs, compatibility, dimensions, materials, pricing context, warranty, shipping, returns, and support.
- Brand/entity information: who the company is, what it sells, where it serves, differentiators, evidence, and trust signals.
- Comparison and selection guidance.
- FAQ content written as direct answers.
- Authoritative guides that connect questions to products and collections.

## AI Crawler Accessibility

Check whether important content is available in server-rendered HTML and not hidden behind blocked resources, client-only rendering, or interaction-only panels.

Define requirements for:

- Crawlable product facts.
- Crawlable collection descriptions.
- Crawlable FAQ content.
- Accessible internal links.
- Clear canonical URLs.

## llms.txt Strategy

Recommend `llms.txt` only as a strategic discovery aid. It should summarize important store resources, not replace sitemap, structured data, or crawlable HTML.

Suggested content areas:

- Brand overview.
- Product categories.
- Key buying guides.
- Support and warranty pages.
- Important comparison resources.

Implementation owner for file/theme changes: `shopify-frontend-code-writer`.

## Entity Understanding

Improve entity clarity with:

- Consistent brand name and organization details.
- Product category taxonomy.
- SameAs profiles when appropriate.
- Structured data requirements.
- Clear about/contact/support pages.

## GEO Output

For each recommendation, state:

- AI visibility issue.
- Why an AI system may fail to understand or cite the store.
- Required content or technical change.
- Affected Shopify surface.
- Implementation owner.
