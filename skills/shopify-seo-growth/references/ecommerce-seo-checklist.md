# Ecommerce SEO Checklist

## Product Pages

Evaluate:

- Product title alignment with commercial search intent.
- Meta title uniqueness and clarity.
- Meta description usefulness for click-through.
- H1 presence and uniqueness.
- Product information completeness: specs, use cases, compatibility, dimensions, materials, warranty, shipping, returns, FAQs.
- Internal links to related products, collections, guides, and support resources.
- Image alt text requirements based on product/entity clarity.
- Review visibility and review schema eligibility.
- Out-of-stock handling: preserve ranking when restock is likely, redirect only when permanently discontinued.

## Collection Pages

Evaluate:

- Collection title and H1 alignment with category intent.
- Intro copy that helps users and search engines understand category scope.
- Collection hierarchy and internal linking.
- Product grid crawlability.
- Filter/index strategy.
- Pagination discoverability.
- Canonical behavior for sorted, filtered, and tagged URLs.

Filtering SEO risks:

- Indexable low-value filter combinations.
- Duplicate titles/descriptions across filtered URLs.
- Important subcategories trapped only as filters.
- Faceted URLs receiving internal links without indexation strategy.

## Blog and Content

Evaluate:

- Topic cluster coverage.
- Internal links from informational content to commercial collections/products.
- Search intent match.
- Article freshness and evidence.
- Author/entity credibility where relevant.
- FAQ opportunities based on real customer questions.

## Organic Growth Prioritization

Prioritize by:

1. Revenue relevance.
2. Search demand.
3. Indexation/rich-result eligibility.
4. Implementation effort.
5. Confidence.

Always separate content requirements from implementation owner.
