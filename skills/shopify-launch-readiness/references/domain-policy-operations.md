# Domain, Policy and Operations

## Domain and Access

- Primary domain resolves to the intended store.
- SSL is valid and redirects are intentional.
- Canonical host behavior is consistent.
- Storefront password and maintenance states match launch timing.
- Old domains and important URLs have a migration or redirect plan.

## Policies and Trust

- Privacy, terms, refund and shipping policies are published and reachable where required.
- Contact details and support channels are real and monitored.
- Footer and checkout links point to the correct documents.
- Placeholder names, addresses, emails and template text are absent.
- Legal adequacy remains a human-owner decision.

## Customer Accounts and Notifications

- Account mode is intentional and login routes work.
- New and returning customer journeys are understood.
- Sender identity and customer notification templates are correct.
- Order, fulfillment, cancellation and refund communications have owners.

## Analytics, Consent and Apps

- Required analytics and conversion events have current evidence.
- Duplicate pixels and conflicting app tracking are flagged.
- Consent behavior matches the merchant's approved policy and markets.
- Essential app embeds, webhooks and integrations are enabled in the target theme or store.
- Analytics depth should be delegated when a specialist owner exists.

## SEO Baseline

- Storefront is crawlable when launch requires indexing.
- Password and noindex states are intentional.
- Robots, sitemap, canonical and metadata basics are available.
- Search Console and Merchant Center readiness is recorded when in scope.
- Deep analysis belongs to `shopify-seo-growth`.

## Operations and Handoff

- Inventory, fulfillment, refund, support and escalation owners are named.
- Staff access follows least privilege and required operators can sign in.
- Backup, release rollback and incident contacts are documented.
- Merchant staff know how to operate orders, content and critical apps.
- Post-launch monitoring windows and issue channels are defined.
