# Shopify Launch Checklist

## Context

- [ ] Exact store and target theme resolved
- [ ] Primary domain and launch markets resolved
- [ ] Launch type, date and business model recorded
- [ ] Access limits and evidence timestamp recorded

## Commerce

- [ ] Products, variants, pricing and sales channels verified
- [ ] Inventory locations and policy verified
- [ ] Collections and navigation verified
- [ ] Markets, languages and currencies verified
- [ ] Shipping zones and rates verified
- [ ] Taxes and duties have owner confirmation
- [ ] Payments and checkout have current evidence
- [ ] Discounts, subscriptions or gift cards tested when applicable

## Storefront and Trust

- [ ] Primary customer flows have storefront QA evidence
- [ ] Desktop, tablet and mobile critical paths covered
- [ ] Domain, SSL, redirects and password state verified
- [ ] Policies, contact and support paths accessible
- [ ] Customer accounts and notifications verified
- [ ] No placeholder or test content exposed

## Discoverability and Data

- [ ] Crawlability and indexation baseline verified
- [ ] Search Console and Merchant Center scope recorded
- [ ] Analytics, pixels and consent evidence recorded
- [ ] Essential apps and integrations verified

## Operations

- [ ] Fulfillment, support, refund and escalation owners named
- [ ] Staff access and handoff complete
- [ ] Backup, rollback and incident plan recorded
- [ ] Post-launch monitoring owner and window defined

## Gate

- [ ] Every check has a valid status
- [ ] Every P0/P1 has evidence, impact, action and owner
- [ ] Manual decisions are explicit
- [ ] Accepted risks have owner, deadline and contingency
- [ ] Final gate follows the documented decision rules
