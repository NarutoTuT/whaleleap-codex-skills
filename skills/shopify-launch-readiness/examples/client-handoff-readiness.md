# Client Handoff Readiness Example

## Scenario

Development is complete, but the merchant will own catalog, orders and support after handoff.

## Additional Evidence

- Merchant staff can access the intended Admin roles.
- Inventory and fulfillment locations have named owners.
- Refund, cancellation and support processes are documented.
- Policy approval is attributed to the merchant or legal owner.
- Essential app subscriptions and external accounts have owners.
- Domain, email sender and analytics ownership are transferred.
- Backup, rollback, launch-day monitoring and escalation contacts are recorded.
- Post-launch monitoring and escalation contacts are recorded.

## Gate Rule

Do not issue `GO` when the storefront technically works but no accountable owner can operate orders, fulfillment, refunds or customer support.
