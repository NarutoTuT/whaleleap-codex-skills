# Humanizer

Use this skill to make copy clearer, warmer, and more believable without making it fluffy.

## Principles

- Prefer concrete benefits over vague hype.
- Keep sentences varied, direct, and easy to scan.
- Remove filler, cliches, inflated claims, and generic AI phrasing.
- Preserve the user's intent, brand position, and factual boundaries.
- For conversion copy, answer the buyer's real doubts: quality, fit, delivery, support, risk, and value.
- For premium brands, sound confident and calm rather than loud.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, context, and desired action.
2. Keep the strongest claim, then make it specific.
3. Replace generic adjectives with sensory, practical, or outcome-based details.
4. Tighten CTAs so they describe the action clearly.
5. Return copy in the format the user needs: headline options, section copy, PDP blocks, FAQs, microcopy, or full page copy.

## Voice Defaults

- Clear, polished, modern.
- Warm but not sentimental.
- Persuasive but not pushy.
- Specific enough to feel written for this product or business.
